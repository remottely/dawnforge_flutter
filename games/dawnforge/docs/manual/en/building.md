# Putting things down

Some of what you make is not a tool. It is a thing that belongs in the world —
a smelter, a workshop, a bridge over water.

## Holding a blueprint

Put it in your item bar and pick it. A **see-through copy** appears where it
would land, and it follows your pointer.

- **Green** means it can go there.
- **Red** means it cannot.

## Putting it down

**Click** or **tap** where the green copy is. It lands on that square and the
thing leaves your hand.

## Why red happens

A square says no for a reason, every time:

- Something is already standing there.
- **You** are standing there, or an animal is. Nothing can be built on top of
  someone.
- The ground underneath will not hold it — deep water, a cliff.
- You are too far away. Walk closer.

## Bridges are different

A bridge does not sit **on** a square. It **becomes** the square: water turns
into something you can walk across, and it stays that way.

## Not yet

What you place disappears if you walk far enough away and come back — the
world does not remember it yet. Nothing you build is safe to leave until it
does.
