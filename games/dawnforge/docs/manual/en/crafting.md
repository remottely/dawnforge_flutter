# Making things

Some things you find. Most things you make.

## With your own two hands

Open your backpack and press **Make**, at the top left. A window opens with
everything you can make with nothing but your hands.

You start the game with a copper pickaxe, and that one tool opens everything
else: mine copper ore and coal, punch a palm tree for logs, and your hands can
make a **smelter** and a **workshop**. Put those down, and they make the rest.

## At a bench

Walk up to a smelter or a workshop and press **E**, or **tap it**. If you are
too far away, nothing happens — take a step closer.

The window looks the same as the one your hands use, because it is the same
window. A bench only makes its own things, and it makes them **faster** than
you can.

## Picking what to make

The left side is everything this bench can make. Pick one and the right side
tells you the rest:

- **What you need**, one line per thing, written as *how many you have* of
  *how many you need*. Green means you have enough. Orange means you do not.
- **What comes out**, and how many.
- **How long it takes** at this bench.

## How many

Press **+** and **−** to ask for more or fewer, or **All** to ask for as many
as your bags can pay for. You can never ask for more than you can pay for, and
you can never ask for more than a hundred at once.

## Making them

Press **Make it** and a bar starts filling. **The world does not stop.** Night
still falls, animals still walk, and you can close the window and come back —
the bench keeps working without you watching.

Things made at a bench land **on the ground in front of it**. Things made by
your hands go **straight into your backpack**, and if your backpack is full
they fall at your feet.

## Changing your mind

Press **Stop** and everything the batch has not used up yet comes straight
back to you. You never lose what you had not spent.

## Not yet

Nothing tells you out loud when a batch finishes — no little message pops up.
For now, the bar is the thing to watch.
