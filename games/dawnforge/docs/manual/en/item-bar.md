# The Item Bar

At the bottom of the screen there is a bar of pockets. That is your **item
bar**, and it is the front of your backpack: the part you can see and reach
without stopping.

One pocket always has a bright yellow edge. That is the pocket you are
**holding from**. Whatever sits in it is what you have in your hand.

## Choosing a pocket

Three ways, and they all do the same thing:

- **Press a number key.** `1` is the first pocket of the bar, `2` the second,
  and so on. `0` is the tenth.
- **Tap a pocket** with your finger or the mouse.
- **Press `Q` or `E`** to step the yellow edge one pocket to the left or to
  the right. It wraps around: past the last pocket it comes back to the first.

## Turning the page

Your backpack has thirty pockets and the bar shows ten. The dots under the bar
tell you how many pages there are and which one you are looking at. Press
**Page Up** or **Page Down** to turn to another page. It goes in a circle:
past the last page you land on the first.

When you turn the page, the yellow edge stays in the same spot on the new
page — so the number keys always mean the same position, whatever page you
are on.

## What the pockets show

A pocket with something in it shows a small picture of it. If you have more
than one of the same thing, the number in the corner tells you how many. One
of a thing shows no number: one apple just looks like an apple.

Empty pockets are empty on purpose. Things you pick up fill them in from the
left.

## Not yet

You cannot open the full backpack yet, and you cannot move things from one
pocket to another. That screen is being built. For now the bar shows you what
you are carrying and lets you choose what to hold.
