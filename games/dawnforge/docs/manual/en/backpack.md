# The Backpack

Press **I** or **Tab** to open your backpack. Press **Esc** — or **I** again —
to close it.

All thirty pockets are there, in six rows of five. The item bar at the bottom
of the screen shows ten of these same pockets at a time; it is the same
backpack seen through a smaller window, not a second one.

## The world keeps going

Look behind the backpack. The trees are still there, blurred, and things are
still moving. **The game does not stop when you open a screen.** You cannot
walk or use anything while the backpack is open, but time does not wait for
you — so do not open it to hide from something.

## Moving things around

**Drag a thing and drop it on another pocket.** Three things can happen, and
the game picks the right one:

- **The pocket is empty** — the whole pile moves there.
- **The pocket has the same thing** — the piles join. If they do not all fit,
  what is left stays where it was.
- **The pocket has something else** — the two swap places.

**Hold Shift while you drag** to move only half the pile. Seven becomes four
and three: when a pile does not split evenly, the half you are carrying gets
the extra one.

## Putting something down

**Drag a thing out of the backpack and drop it anywhere outside** — on the
blurred world. The whole pile lands on the ground at your feet.

It will not jump straight back to you. Anything dropped rests for half a second
before it can be picked up again, so you have time to walk away. After that, if
you come close, it flies back to you like anything else on the ground.

## Not yet

The **Sort** button is not here. It is being kept out on purpose until the game
knows how to rank tools against food against building blocks — a Sort button
that puts things in an order that changes later is worse than no button.
