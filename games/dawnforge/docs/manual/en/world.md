# The World

The world of Dawnforge does not end. Walk in any direction, as far as you
want — there is always more land ahead. The game builds the ground in front
of you while you walk, and tidies away the ground far behind you. You never
see it happen.

Every world is made from a **seed**. A seed is a number. The same seed
always makes the same world, down to the last tile. A new game rolls a new
seed, so every new world is a surprise.

What you will see:

- **Grass** — the natural ground of the forest. You start on it.
- **Water** — the sea. It is the blue all around the land.
- **Mountains** — dark, stepped ground. They rise like stairs: one step,
  two steps, three.

You always start on a flat, safe patch of grass, with open space all
around you.
